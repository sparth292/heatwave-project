DATA SOURCES & STAKEHOLDERS

Data Sources and Stakeholder Roles

Data Sources

The following sources provide the simulated and real-time data used by the Heatwave Intelligence System:

  • AWS Sensors — Automated weather stations across the region collecting temperature, humidity, and heat index data.
  • IMD Database — India Meteorological Department records and historical data for validated observations.
  • Satellite Feeds — Orbital sensor data providing regional thermal imagery and large-scale pattern analysis.

Forecast & Alert Workflow

  1. Data ingestion from AWS sensors and satellite feeds
  2. Processing and heat index calculation
  3. Risk category assignment using project-defined thresholds
  4. Alert generation and dissemination
  5. Public advisory and precautionary guidance

Stakeholder Roles

Disaster Management Office
Issue official warnings, coordinate emergency response, and manage evacuation procedures if required.
Student Welfare Department
Monitor student well-being, adjust academic schedules during extreme heat, and provide hydration facilities.
Faculty Advisory Committee
Review risk advisories, approve precautionary measures, and communicate with teaching staff.
Library & IT Services
Ensure system availability, manage data archival, and support user access to recorded observations.

Abbreviation Guide

AWS
Ground-based instrumentation measuring temperature, humidity, wind, and other meteorological parameters.
IMD
National agency responsible for meteorological observations, weather forecasting, and seismology.
HW
Extended period of excessively hot weather, typically with high humidity, posing health risks.
SHW
Heatwave conditions exceeding official thresholds with potentially severe health impacts.
