ABOUT THE SYSTEM

Heatwave Intelligence System

Objective

To provide a comprehensive heatwave monitoring and awareness portal for the Somaiya Vidyavihar University community, delivering real-time simulated conditions, risk assessments, and practical safety advisories.

“Our mission is to educate and empower students and staff with knowledge about heat risks and preventive measures. Awareness saves lives during extreme heat events.”

— Chief Meteorologist, Department of Computer Engineering

Data Sources

The system integrates data from multiple authoritative sources including automated weather stations, satellite observations, and national meteorological databases to ensure comprehensive coverage.

Stakeholders

Key stakeholders include the Disaster Management Office, Student Welfare Department, Faculty Advisory Committee, and the broader Somaiya Vidyavihar University community.
